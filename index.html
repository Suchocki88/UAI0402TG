State Standards Alignment

Understanding AI: A Conceptual Approach
Chapter 4, Lesson 4.1: What Is Bias?

Lesson 4.1 is a foundational Lesson Day where students explore human cognitive bias before examining AI bias. Students learn four types of bias (confirmation, availability, implicit, in-group), analyze everyday scenarios, and reflect on their own biases. This lesson emphasizes metacognition, critical thinking, collaborative discussion, and self-reflection—competencies addressed across multiple state and national standards frameworks.
